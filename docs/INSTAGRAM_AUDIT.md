## What changed

- Symphony's declared project cover now drives both the homepage hero and its project page. Clicking the hero opens Symphony.
- The homepage renders all seven documented project records, rather than a four-project shortlist. The full archive retains category filters.
- Every image has a descriptive Persian caption and a photo / render / construction classification. Eight additional Symphony photographs cover the lobby, pool, stone/glass stair and entrance/construction details; all were visually inspected. Images showing installation or scaffolding are explicitly marked as construction-stage photographs.
- The collaborative Pardis poster is linked and credited to ARV Studio. It remains a labelled design visualization, not a completed-building photo; the small preview is not upscaled.
- The source post and tagged location are linked from each matched project page. Persian editorial descriptions follow the existing understated form/function/comfort/view/permanence values; hashtags, campaigns and visitor replies are not copied into the site.
- Instagram publication dates are not used as completion dates. Existing owner-provided project dates remain distinct.
